\subsubsection{Analysis}

In Pucks speech, in Scene two of act two, from "A Midsummernight's dream" by
Shakespare, Pucks speaks about how he does not now who the "Athenian" is. By
using a different meter, a poem structure and also utillizing a diffrent
metrical foot, Shakespare, or Pucks, creats a contrast between the magical
world, where Pucks lives, and the \quotation{real} World.
